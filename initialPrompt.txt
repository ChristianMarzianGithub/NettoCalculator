# Task: Build a German Gross-to-Net Salary Calculator with Minijob Support

You are an experienced full-stack developer with knowledge of German payroll taxation.

Build a fully working, responsive web application that calculates the net income of an employee in Germany from their gross salary, including the option to add income from a Minijob.

The application should work similarly to https://www.brutto-netto-rechner.info/ but with a cleaner interface and combined-income calculations.

**IMPORTANT: This is a one-shot implementation. Implement everything autonomously. Do not ask clarifying questions, do not stop after planning, and do not require me to approve individual file changes. Create the project, install dependencies, implement the application, and run all available tests.**

## 2. Main functionality

### Regular employment

The user enters their gross salary and the application calculates their net income.

Inputs:

- Gross salary amount
- Monthly or yearly salary selection
- Tax year (default 2026)
- Tax class (Steuerklasse I–VI)
- Federal state (Bundesland)
- Church tax (yes/no)
- Number of children and relevant child allowances
- Childless long-term care insurance surcharge eligibility
- Age or birth year
- Health insurance: statutory (GKV) or private (PKV)
- Statutory health insurance additional contribution rate (Zusatzbeitrag)
- Private health insurance contributions and relevant employer subsidy, if PKV is selected
- Pension insurance obligation
- Unemployment insurance obligation
- Optional annual bonus or one-time payments

Show:

- Gross monthly salary
- Wage tax (Lohnsteuer)
- Solidarity surcharge (Solidaritätszuschlag)
- Church tax (Kirchensteuer)
- Health insurance (Krankenversicherung)
- Pension insurance (Rentenversicherung)
- Unemployment insurance (Arbeitslosenversicherung)
- Long-term care insurance (Pflegeversicherung)
- Total deductions
- Net monthly salary
- Net annual salary

Also display the employee's total effective deduction percentage.

### Minijob functionality

Include an optional section named "Zusätzlicher Minijob".

Inputs:

- Enable/disable Minijob
- Monthly Minijob gross income
- Pension insurance exemption (Rentenversicherungspflicht befreit: yes/no)
- Minijob taxation method: employer-paid flat-rate tax or individual taxation using payroll tax characteristics

Implement the applicable German Minijob rules for the selected tax year.

For 2026, validate the applicable regular monthly Minijob earnings limit, currently €603.

Important considerations:

- An eligible first Minijob alongside one social-insurance-liable main employment generally remains exempt from employee health, long-term care and unemployment insurance contributions.
- The employee generally pays 3.6% pension insurance in a commercial Minijob unless exempt, subject to applicable minimum-contribution rules.
- Under employer-paid flat-rate taxation, the 2% flat-rate tax is generally an employer expense and must not automatically be deducted from the employee's net income.
- Individual taxation must be handled separately, without pretending it is always tax-free.
- Handle earnings above the Minijob limit correctly, distinguishing occasional permitted fluctuations from regular employment and indicating when an arrangement no longer qualifies as a Minijob.
- For the MVP, assume exactly one commercial Minijob alongside one regular employment. Clearly document exclusions, such as private-household Minijobs, multiple simultaneous Minijobs, and Midijob calculations.
- Do not simply add Minijob gross income to the normal taxable salary in the standard flat-rate-tax scenario.

Display:

- Minijob gross monthly income
- Employee pension contributions, if applicable
- Employee tax withholding, if applicable
- Minijob net monthly income
- Combined net monthly income
- Combined net annual income

## 5. Calculation correctness

Accuracy matters more than visual complexity.

Research and use authoritative German sources, including:

- Bundesministerium der Finanzen (BMF) for official payroll-tax calculation algorithms
- Official Programmablaufplan (PAP) for wage-tax calculations
- Deutsche Rentenversicherung
- Minijob-Zentrale
- Official social insurance contribution rates and contribution ceilings

Requirements:

1. Implement wage-tax calculation using the official BMF payroll-tax algorithm for the relevant tax year, rather than a rough percentage approximation.
2. Apply German social insurance contribution rates, contribution ceilings, and relevant statutory exceptions.
3. Correctly apply church-tax rates depending on the state.
4. Implement solidarity surcharge calculation and exemption thresholds.
5. Account for relevant child-related care-insurance rules, including Saxony's special treatment.
6. Clearly distinguish employee contributions from employer contributions.
7. Keep tax rates, thresholds, contribution ceilings and calculation constants in a separate configuration module grouped by tax year.
8. If a tax year cannot be implemented accurately, disable it rather than silently producing estimated results.
9. Clearly state that this is an informational calculator, not legally binding payroll advice.

Do not invent German tax rules or contribution rates.

If necessary, use a reputable existing library with verifiable tax-year support, but document its source, license, assumptions and limitations. Do not use an unverified generic flat income-tax formula.
